# GYM X — Website Section Guide

A plain-English, section-by-section description of the GYM X website. This document describes **what each part of the page is, what it shows, and how it behaves** — it contains no code.

> Note on branding: the site is a rebranded/adapted version of an earlier "Body Factory Bali" template, now presented as **GYM X** (Karachi). Some non-visible metadata (search-engine structured data, image alt text, social aria-labels) may still carry the older name; the visible page content uses GYM X / Karachi.

---

## Page Structure Overview

The page is a single, long scrolling homepage. From top to bottom it contains:

1. Top Navigation Bar (header)
2. Hero Banner (image carousel)
3. Inclusive Approach (intro / about)
4. What We Do (three feature cards)
5. Meet Your Coaches (team carousel)
6. BFB Events (event cards carousel)
7. Footer (contact, social, navigation, scroll-to-top)

---

## 1. Top Navigation Bar (Header)

**Purpose:** Primary site navigation, always visible at the top of the page.

**Contents (left to right):**
- **Brand / Logo area** — displays the site name **"GYM X"** as text.
- **Main menu links:**
  - Home (highlighted as the current/active page)
  - Classes
  - Recover
  - Memberships
  - Meal Plans
  - STAYS
  - Property Investment
- **Action buttons:**
  - "Get Started" — a call-to-action button.
  - "Login" — a button with a small login/exit icon, styled as the primary (brand-colour) button.

**Behaviour & layout notes:**
- The full horizontal menu is designed for larger (desktop/extra-large) screens.
- On smaller screens the menu links are hidden; a compact mobile navigation area exists in that space (a hamburger-style control), so mobile visitors are not left without navigation.
- Menu items use hover effects (background highlight and a small downward arrow indicator).

---

## 2. Hero Banner (Image Carousel)

**Purpose:** The large, full-height opening visual that gives the site its first impression.

**Contents:**
- A **full-screen background image slideshow (carousel)** made up of several gym/atmosphere photos.
- Overlaid on top, centred:
  - A large headline: **"WELCOME TO"** followed by **"BODY FACTORY KARACHI"**.
  - A **"GET STARTED"** button.
- On the right-hand side (larger screens only): a **Google reviews panel** showing:
  - "We Love our community"
  - "1,616 Google reviews | 4.4"
  - A row of star icons.
- At the bottom of the hero: **Previous / Next** round navigation buttons (left/right chevrons) for moving through the slides.

**Behaviour:**
- The hero is a carousel of multiple images; navigation arrows let the visitor move between slides.

---

## 3. Inclusive Approach (Intro / About)

**Purpose:** Introduces the gym's philosophy and brand promise.

**Contents:**
- A decorative **icon / logo mark** at the top.
- A two-part heading:
  - **"INCLUSIVE APPROACH"**
  - **"TO LIFESTYLE & FITNESS"**
- Three short paragraphs of body text explaining the gym's all-inclusive philosophy, its four core values **(Sleep, Perform, Nourish, Restore)**, and its community-focused identity.
- Two buttons:
  - **"Join Us Today"** (primary button)
  - **"Learn More"** (outlined secondary button)
- On larger screens, a **supporting lifestyle image** is shown alongside the text.

**Layout notes:** Text and image sit side-by-side on wide screens and stack vertically (text first) on small screens.

---

## 4. What We Do (Feature Cards)

**Purpose:** Showcases the main offerings through three visual cards.

**Contents:**
- A section heading:
  - **"WHAT WE DO"**
  - **"A 360° APPROACH TO HEALTH & FITNESS"**
- Three large cards, each with a title bar, a photo, and a **"LEARN MORE"** button:
  1. **Perform** — training/physical performance offering.
  2. **Property** — property/real-estate offering.
  3. **Meal Plans** — nutrition/meal-plan offering.

**Behaviour:**
- Cards have a hover effect: the title bar colour flips (from dark to light) and the "LEARN MORE" button slides into view.
- On wide screens the three cards sit in a row; on smaller screens they stack.

## 5. Meet Your Coaches (Team Carousel)

**Purpose:** Introduces the gym's coaching team.

**Contents:**
- A decorative icon and a centred heading: **"MEET YOUR COACHES"**.
- A **horizontal carousel of coach cards**. Each card shows:
  - A square profile photo.
  - The coach's **name**.
  - The coach's **speciality** (e.g. Strength & Conditioning, Yoga, Muay Thai, Pilates, Functional Training).
  - A **"View Profile"** link.
- Named coaches in the current content include: Ashleigh, Oli, Tommy, Becky, Mei, Stella, Ika Dewi, Komang, William, Gege, and Konstantinus — covering specialities such as Strength & Conditioning, Metabolic Conditioning, Body Building, Yoga, Pilates, Mobility, Rehabilitation, Functional Training, and Muay Thai.
- Below the carousel: **Previous slide / Next slide** arrow controls, and a **"See All Team Members"** button.

**Layout notes:** The number of cards visible at once changes with screen width (roughly one on mobile up to four on very wide screens); the rest are reached via the arrows.

## 6. BFB Events (Event Cards Carousel)

**Purpose:** Promotes recurring community events and activities.

**Contents:**
- A decorative icon and a centred heading: **"BFB EVENTS"**.
- A **carousel of event cards**, each with a large photo, an event name, and a schedule/button label. Cards include:
  - **The Snap Spot** — "Every Fri"
  - **LADIES DAY** — "Every Sat, Sun"
  - **Sunday Grill & Chill** — "Every Sun"
- Below the cards: **Previous slide / Next slide** arrow controls.

**Layout notes:** Cards are shown side-by-side on larger screens and one-at-a-time on mobile, navigated with the arrows.

**Purpose:** Closing section with contact details, social links, site navigation, and trust badges.

**Contents, grouped into columns:**

- **Social icons row:** Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok.
  - The **Facebook** icon is the only link that navigates away — it opens the GYM X Facebook page.
  - The other social icons are shown for branding but are not active links.

- **Contact block:**

  - **Opening Hours:** "6am – 10pm all week".
  - **Email:** info@gymx.com (with an envelope icon).
  - **Phone:** 03121057858 (with a phone icon).

- **Company line:** "BF LIFESTYLE GROUP PTE. LTD."

- **Awards / badges row:** Several small award badge images (trust/credibility markers).

- **Quick-links (two columns):**
  - Column 1: Home, Classes, Coaches, Recover, Property, Career, Blogs
  - Column 2: Terms & Conditions, My Account, Meal Plans, Cafe, Events, Contact Us
  - These are presented as text links; on the adapted site they are displayed but not actively navigable (except where noted).

- **Scroll-to-top control:** A small circular button at the bottom that returns the visitor to the top of the page.

## Cross-Cutting Notes

- **Single-page layout:** Everything above lives on one continuously scrolling homepage.
- **Responsiveness:** The design adjusts across screen sizes — multi-column layouts collapse to single columns on phones, and carousels reduce the number of visible items. Sections use consistent, full-width bands separated by generous spacing.
- **Visual style:** Clean, modern fitness aesthetic — full-bleed photography in the hero and cards, uppercase display headings, and a brand accent colour used on buttons and highlights.
- **Interactive elements:** Hero and section carousels (previous/next controls), card hover effects, and the scroll-to-top button.
